AZWEC Show Calendar

AZWEC hosts both Schooling Shows and official USAWE Licensed Shows throughout the year. Here's what each type offers and how the scores count.

AZWEC Schooling Shows

Levels 1–4 · Judged by a local Arizona judge

  • Scores count toward AZWEC Year End Awards
  • Qualifying scores for the AZWEC Finals each December

USAWE Licensed Shows organized by AZWEC

All levels · Judged by a USAWE approved judge

  • Scores count toward AZWEC Year End Awards
  • Qualifying scores for the AZWEC Finals each December
  • USAWE qualifying scores for Regional Finals
  • USAWE Year End Awards

Other Licensed & Schooling Shows

Not organized by AZWEC · Listed for planning

  • Scores do not count toward AZWEC Year End Awards
  • Scores do not count as AZWEC Finals qualifying scores
  • Included to help members plan their show season
